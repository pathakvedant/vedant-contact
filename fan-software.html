TRAASS Fan Pro: Centrifugal Fan Design Software

TRAASS Fan Pro is centrifugal fan design software by Vedant Pathak (VP): from the duty point to the wheel, blades, shaft, bearings, motor, start-up and casing, for industrial fans in clean air, dust and hot gas.

What the centrifugal fan software designs

  • Fan selection: size, speed and wheel type (backward curved, backward inclined, airfoil, radial tip, radial, paddle, forward curved) for your flow, pressure, temperature, altitude and dust
  • Aerodynamics: velocity triangles, slip, losses, performance curve, stall margin and the operating point on your system curve
  • Impeller strength: blade, backplate and shroud thickness, with stress checked along the whole blade, overspeed, temperature and wear allowance
  • Shaft and critical speed: stepped shaft, deflection, fatigue and first critical speed with margin
  • Bearings and housings: spherical roller or ball bearings and SNL plummer blocks picked from maker catalogue data, life, lubrication and regreasing interval
  • Motor and start-up: motor from catalogue data, start time for direct-on-line, star-delta, soft starter and VFD, hot and cold gas, damper open or closed
  • Casing, hub and accessories: volute, plate and stiffeners, hub and key, inlet box, damper and silencer
  • Balance and vibration: balance grade and vibration limits by fan category
  • Outputs: CAD drawings (DXF, SVG, STL, STEP), an Excel calculation sheet and a Word report

Built on published engineering

The calculations follow textbook fluid mechanics and strength of materials, AMCA fan standards and bearing and motor maker catalogue data, and each method is checked against its source. Every value the software picks can be overridden by the engineer.

Who it is for

Fan manufacturers, APCD and dust collection companies, consultants and plant engineers who design or check centrifugal fans and blowers.
