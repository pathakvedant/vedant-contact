APCD Consultant: Air Pollution Control System Consulting

Vedant Pathak (VP) of TRAASS is an APCD consultant: an air pollution control system consultant for plants in India. Application study, root cause analysis of existing dust collection systems, and selection of the best-suited air pollution control device and fan, based on 5+ years in the field.

What an APCD (air pollution control system) consultant does

An APCD consultant, or air pollution control system consultant, studies your process and dust, finds why an existing system is underperforming, and recommends the air pollution control device that meets the emission norm reliably: bag filter, cyclone, wet scrubber or ESP, with the right fan.

What I help with

  • Application study: dust type, particle size, temperature, moisture, airflow and emission limits
  • Root cause analysis: low suction, high differential pressure, bag failure, leakage and fan vibration
  • Equipment selection: bag filter, cyclone, wet scrubber or ESP
  • Fan selection: duty point, static pressure, efficiency and motor sizing, using fan design software
  • Proof with data: before and after readings in a dashboard
