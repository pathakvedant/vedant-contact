B2B Marketing & Business Development for Manufacturers

Vedant Pathak (VP) of TRAASS helps manufacturing companies get found, trusted and chosen through digital marketing, branding, business development and client conversion, measured at every funnel stage.

Who this is for

Manufacturers and industrial suppliers who rely on a few relationships for orders, and want a steadier flow of qualified enquiries.

Marketing and branding

  • Brand and positioning: a clear promise, consistent identity, catalogues and company profiles
  • Digital marketing: website, search visibility, LinkedIn and email campaigns
  • Technical content: case studies and application notes that buyers trust
  • Events: trade-show planning and follow-up

Business development

  • Market mapping: target industries, accounts and decision makers
  • Prospecting: outreach that opens technical conversations
  • Techno-commercial consulting: site visits and the right solution
  • Proposals and closing: offers, follow-up and negotiation
  • Pipeline and CRM: lead scoring, conversion tracking and forecasting

Why an engineer doing this helps

Industrial buyers want to know you understand their plant. A B.Tech Mechanical background and 5+ years with APCD customers means the conversation starts with their problem, and a data analyst's habits mean every channel gets measured in enquiries and orders.

How the work runs

  • Find the right accounts
  • Qualify early
  • Consult before selling
  • Propose around the outcome
  • Close with disciplined follow-up
  • Turn each result into proof for the next client
