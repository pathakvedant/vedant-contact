How I consult on dust collection systems

Study the application

Dust type, particle size, temperature, moisture, airflow and emission norms.

RCA of the existing system

Suction loss, high ΔP, bag choking, leakage, fan vibration: find the true cause.

Pick the best-suited APCD

Bag filter, cyclone, wet scrubber or ESP, with the right fan on its curve.

Prove it with data

Before/after readings, energy savings and compliance, all in a dashboard.

About Vedant Pathak (VP) & TRAASS

Quick answers for anyone looking for a B2B marketer, business developer, APCD expert or business analyst in the mechanical domain.

Who is Vedant Pathak (VP)?

Vedant Pathak, also known as VP, is the founder of TRAASS: a B.Tech Mechanical Engineer and Certified Data Analyst working as a Business Analyst in the mechanical domain, working across industries from steel to FMCG, with experience in B2B marketing, business development and 5+ years in air pollution control devices (APCD).

What is TRAASS?

TRAASS is Vedant Pathak's practice for B2B marketing, business development, business analysis, APCD and dust collection consulting, dashboards, ERP implementation and digital transformation for companies in every industry, from steel to FMCG.

What B2B marketing does Vedant Pathak do for manufacturers?

Digital marketing, branding and positioning, LinkedIn and email campaigns, technical content and catalogues, search visibility, and funnel analytics, all aimed at turning industrial buyers into qualified enquiries.

What business development work does Vedant Pathak do?

Market and account mapping, prospecting, techno-commercial consulting with buyers, proposals, negotiation and closing, CRM and pipeline discipline, and client conversion tracking for manufacturing businesses.

What APCD services does Vedant Pathak offer?

Application study, selection of the best-suited air pollution control device (bag filter, cyclone, wet scrubber or ESP), industrial fan selection, and root cause analysis (RCA) of existing dust collection systems.

What does a business analyst in the mechanical domain do?

A business analyst in the mechanical domain brings engineering knowledge and data analysis together: turning plant, sales and production data into dashboards and decisions, mapping processes for ERP, and leading digital transformation in manufacturing.
